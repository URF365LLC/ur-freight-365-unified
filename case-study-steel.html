17+ months moving steel for Tube Supply — a Triple-S Steel family company.

Every load open-deck. Every load steel, pipe, or metal products — API pipe, OCTG, plate, and specialty metals on flatbed, step deck, and hotshot. This is the freight we are purpose-built to move, and here is what a real lane program looks like.

A lane program, not random freight

80% of volume ran on three lanes:

  • Houston → Odessa, TX — 128 loads, the oilfield backbone
  • Houston → Edmonton, AB — 61 cross-border loads, zero service failures
  • Houston → Midland, TX

Capacity under pressure

Trucks secured through tight markets, holidays, and Blitz Week. 104 carriers sourced across the account — proof of reach into specialized open-deck markets where "no trucks available" is the industry default.

Rate protection, not spot-chasing

When the spot market ran 30–40% higher, we held the customer's known lane rate and adjusted only for fuel — then re-quoted downward when the market cooled. We protect the rate you know, not chase the spot.

Accountability, in writing

One confirmed product issue across the program — and it produced a stricter standard, not an excuse: pre-departure load photos, BOL and piece-count verification at origin, driver shortage confirmation, delivery photos with signed POD, and an incident file the moment anything looks off.

Verified on every load

Loads run with CarrierView load verification: truck cab card and plates, truck & trailer unit photos, empty-trailer photo, load photos and seal, BOL, and POD collected in-system.

The dedicated-lane model

Concentrated lanes let carriers be planned and committed. With a weekly forecast — even tentative — we build a committed carrier bench on your core lanes: fewer scrambles, steadier rates, faster coverage.

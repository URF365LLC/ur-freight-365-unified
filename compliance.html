Every load, documented

Load verification on CarrierView.

Freight moves with third-party load verification through CarrierView — a documented, time-stamped record on the load, not a promise.

  • Truck cab card / registration and license plates
  • Truck & trailer unit photos, plus empty-trailer photo
  • Load photos and seal
  • Bill of Lading (BOL) collected in-system
  • Proof of Delivery (POD) collected in-system
  • Start & end mileage photos

Chain-of-custody standard: pickup photos before departure, BOL and piece-count verified at origin, driver confirmation of any visible shortage, delivery photos with signed POD — and an incident file opened the moment a discrepancy is alleged.

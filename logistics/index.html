FAQ

Common logistics and cross-border questions.

What does UR Freight 365 coordinate?

Freight intake and communication for port, warehouse, cross-dock, cross-border, and last-mile moves, with the right partner path based on the shipment.

What details help port or warehouse moves?

Origin, facility name, port or warehouse reference, destination, commodity, pallet count, weight, timing, equipment, and handling notes.

What about cross-border freight?

Share U.S.–Mexico or U.S.–Canada lane details, border timing, broker or document readiness, equipment needs, and delivery appointment requirements.

How should urgent handoffs be handled?

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how UR Freight 365 LLC handles information submitted through freight quote requests, contact forms, email, and phone communications.

Information We Collect

UR Freight 365 LLC may collect information you choose to provide through contact forms, quote requests, email, phone calls, or freight communications. This can include your name, company, email address, phone number, shipment details, pickup and delivery information, commodity details, timing requirements, and related notes.

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to respond to quote requests, communicate about freight needs, coordinate requested services, improve our site and intake process, and maintain business records related to freight inquiries.

No Sale of Personal Information

UR Freight 365 LLC does not sell personal information to third parties. Information may be shared only as needed to respond to your request, coordinate freight-related communications, comply with law, or protect legitimate business interests.

Data Requests

To request access, correction, deletion, or communication preferences related to information you provided, contact UR Freight 365 LLC at quote@urfreight365llc.com or 346-522-2772.

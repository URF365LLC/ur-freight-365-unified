We already run your site playbook.

Utility-scale solar freight is not general trucking. It is staging-yard call-aheads, appointment windows logged by the logistics supervisor, PPE at the gate, gravel-road rules, and site specs that require anything over 8 feet to ride a flatbed. We have delivered onto live solar sites — and our drivers show up already knowing the drill.

Projects we have delivered to

Felina Solar — Fabens, TX

Deliveries onto a DEPCOM Power–managed utility-scale site: staging at the yard, appointment scheduling with the site logistics supervisor, and gate protocols followed load after load.

Split Rail Solar — Missouri

Scheduled material deliveries under full site protocol — 24–48 hour call-aheads, PPE requirements, and the site's own rule that shipments over 8 feet must move on a flatbed. That is our equipment.

Bowling Green Solar Plant — Missouri

A port-to-site program: 39 loads out of the Port of Galveston delivered to the project, coordinating port pickup, transload, and site appointments end to end.

Southern New Mexico project

~100 loads of project material delivered into a single New Mexico solar site from Gulf and West Coast origins — sustained, scheduled, surge-capable.

How the border works when you ship with us

Customs, handled by professionals

Customs clearance runs through licensed customs brokers such as Cole International. Your paperwork is in specialist hands on both sides of the border.

PARS & PAPS coordination

We coordinate the carrier's PARS number and port of entry northbound, and PAPS southbound — so the truck, the broker, and the border are aligned before the load moves.

Documents move with the load

BOLs, proformas, and permits are collected and sent ahead of dispatch. Border escalation is built in: paperwork pre-checked, escalation at 24 hours, commercial review by 48.

Both directions

Southbound Canadian steel and industrial freight into the U.S., northbound Gulf Coast / Texas project freight into Canada — flatbed, step deck, and dry van.
